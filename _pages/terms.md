## Privacy Policy

The privacy of visitors to this website is important. This page summarizes what this site intentionally collects and what third-party services may receive when pages are loaded.

I do not sell personal data, run ads, or intentionally collect visitor profiles through this website.

### Log Files

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs for security and operational purposes. Those logs can include:

* Internet Protocol addresses (IP)
* Browser and device information
* Date and time of requests
* Referring pages
* Requested URLs

I do not receive these logs as a routine analytics feed.

### Local Storage

The site stores your light/dark theme preference in browser local storage so the chosen theme can persist across visits.

### Third-Party Services

Some pages link to external services such as Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, arXiv, YouTube, or publisher websites. If you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply.

Math, plotting, and diagram scripts are only loaded on pages that explicitly need them.

### Contact

If you have questions about this website or want content updated or removed, contact me through the email listed in the sidebar.
